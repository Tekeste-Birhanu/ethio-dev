# Product Requirements Document

## 1. Overview

- **Product:** EthioDev
- **Status:** Draft
- **Summary:** EthioDev helps beginner and junior Ethiopian developers find project opportunities, connect with collaborators, coordinate work in shared Collabspaces, and share completed work.
- **Target users:** Beginner and junior developers across Ethiopia, including people who have an idea and need teammates and developers looking for practical projects to join.
- **Problem:** Developers may have ideas without teammates or skills without a practical place to apply them. EthioDev connects public project discovery with a clear path to form a team, collaborate, and show the result.

## 2. Goals and scope

- **Goals:**
  - Help developers discover relevant collaboration opportunities and other developers.
  - Let opportunity owners evaluate and recruit interested developers.
  - Give teams a shared place to communicate and coordinate tasks.
  - Make completed projects and their contributors discoverable through the opportunity post.
- **Core modules:**
  - **Discover:** Opportunity and developer discovery, keyword search, and filters.
  - **Opportunity:** Public collaboration post, requests, comments, saved posts, and lifecycle controls.
  - **Chat:** Private conversations, with unread counts and notifications.
  - **Collabspace:** A project team space with its own group chat, members, admins, milestones, and a shared taskboard. Group chat is accessed inside its Collabspace, not from the Chat destination.
- **Constraints:** The first product is a mobile-friendly web app. The interface is in English; members may write profile content and posts in English or Amharic. Members can sign in with email or username and password, or Google OAuth. Email is private and used for authentication only; changing email is out of MVP scope.

## 3. User journeys

- **J-01 — Find an opportunity and join:** A developer discovers or searches opportunities, reviews a post and its owner, sends a collaboration request, and is either accepted into a private chat or added directly to the Collabspace.
- **J-02 — Publish and build a team:** A developer publishes an opportunity, reviews incoming collaboration requests, optionally starts private conversations, invites developers, and manages the linked Collabspace.
- **J-03 — Find and contact a developer:** A member searches the developer directory, views a public profile, and sends a chat request. If accepted, a private conversation opens.
- **J-04 — Work together:** Collabspace members use group chat and a shared milestone-based taskboard to coordinate project work.
- **J-05 — Finish and share work:** The opportunity owner marks the opportunity Completed, optionally adds project links, and the completed post remains available in Discover.

### Business rules

- Opportunity owners alone control opportunity content, comments setting, lifecycle, and deletion. Collabspace admins cannot change those controls.
- The opportunity owner can delete an opportunity at any stage. Deletion is final for discovery. If a linked Collabspace exists, it becomes read-only and retains its history for members who had access when archived. Removed or former members cannot access it.
- Private chats are separate from Collabspaces. A private chat may lead to a Collabspace invitation, but does not itself add a member to a team.
- The private Requests tab lists outgoing requests and invitations in one chronological list. Pending items can be withdrawn; resolved items remain visible as history.
- Each opportunity has one owner. If a Collabspace is created for it, the owner is implicitly an admin of that Collabspace.
- The owner creates a Collabspace from the opportunity itself; this action establishes the explicit link. If the owner chooses Add to Collabspace before creating one, guide them through naming and creating the linked space, then add the applicant. Canceling keeps the request pending. A space is never selected by matching its name.
- Profile email is private and used only for authentication. It is never displayed on a profile or shared through the product.
- Members with a password credential can change their password in account settings. Accounts without an EthioDev password do not show a password-change action. Members cannot change their email in the MVP.
- Opportunity comments are flat. Deleting a comment does not remove other comments.
- Owners and admins can delete only empty milestones. If a milestone still has tasks, those tasks must be moved to another milestone before deletion; deleting a milestone never deletes its tasks.
- Opportunity status values are Open, Paused, and Completed. Paused means recruiting is temporarily stopped; Completed means the project is finished. A deleted opportunity is removed from discovery; its linked Collabspace, if any, becomes archived and read-only.

## 5. Quality requirements

- **Security and privacy:** Authenticate with email/username and password or Google OAuth. Enforce role and membership permissions for protected actions in trusted server-side logic. Keep email private; expose only profile fields intended to be public. A member's access to a Collabspace ends immediately when they leave or are removed.
- **Reliability and recovery:** Prevent duplicate pending invitations and duplicate collaboration requests. Preserve pending user input where practical after validation or recoverable operation errors. Confirm destructive private-chat deletion with a warning before removing the conversation for both participants.
- **Accessibility:** Use semantic controls, keyboard access, visible focus, readable contrast, and accessible names. Do not convey state by color alone.
- **Localization:** The application interface is English. User-authored profile and opportunity text may be English or Amharic.

## 6. Assumptions and open decisions

- **Assumptions:** Members can authenticate with email/username and password or Google OAuth. “Completed profile” means name and username are present. Public opportunity and profile content can be viewed without revealing email.
- **Dependencies / risks:** Google OAuth availability is required for Google sign-in. Public profiles and opportunity discovery require clear user controls over the profile fields shown publicly.
- **Open decisions:** No product decisions currently block drafting the MVP requirements. Technical choices and measurable service targets are documented in the relevant technical and design specifications when selected.
